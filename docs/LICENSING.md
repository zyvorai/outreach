# Licensing and the 30-day trial

- **Trial.** Zyvor Outreach has a 30-day trial from the first launch. You do not need a key during the trial.
- **After 30 days** the app asks for a licence key. Your leads, templates and history are not touched; the window unlocks as soon as a valid key is entered.
- **Price.** INR 14,999 per Mac per year incl. 18% GST in India (about US$151 excl. tax outside India). Team price: INR 11,999 per Mac per year (about US$121 excl. tax), minimum INR 35,997 (about US$363), by invoice. Prices: INR incl. 18% GST in India; US dollars excl. tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable). Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking; international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Custom pricing: contact sales@zyvor.dev.
- **Getting a key.** Buy at [zyvor.dev/buy](https://zyvor.dev/buy) or email [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev?subject=Zyvor%20Outreach%20licence). If you want a longer trial first, ask for a trial key.
- **Entering a key.** **Outreach > Licence…** (or the status line at the bottom of the sidebar), paste the key, then choose Activate.
- **Offline.** Keys are verified on your Mac. The app does not contact a licensing server.
- **Where it is stored.** In your Mac's Keychain, with a protected file in Application Support as a fallback.
