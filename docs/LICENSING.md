# Licensing and the 30-day trial

- **Trial.** Zyvor Outreach is free for 30 days from the first launch. You do not need a key.
- **After 30 days** the app asks for a licence key. Your leads, templates and history are not touched; the window unlocks as soon as a valid key is entered.
- **Getting a key.** Email [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev?subject=Zyvor%20Outreach%20licence). If you want a longer trial first, ask for a trial key.
- **Entering a key.** **Outreach > Licence…** (or the status line at the bottom of the sidebar), paste the key, then choose Activate.
- **Offline.** Keys are verified on your Mac. The app does not contact a licensing server.
- **Where it is stored.** In your Mac's Keychain, with a protected file in Application Support as a fallback.
