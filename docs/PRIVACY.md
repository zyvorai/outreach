# Privacy

- The app does not check licences online. Keys are verified on your Mac.
- Your trial start date and licence key are kept in your Keychain.
- Zyvor Outreach is a console. It shows and acts on the leads and messages in the outreach folder on your Mac. We do not receive that data.
- Exports and the action log are plain text and can contain company names and email addresses. Treat them as business data.
