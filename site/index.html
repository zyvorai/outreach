Safe by default

It would rather
not send, than send wrong.

Dry run first

Every send is a preview unless you say otherwise. A pre-flight checklist and a 60-second cancel window sit between you and Send.

Automatic pause

If the bounce rate passes 5%, or a spam complaint arrives, sending stops on its own and tells you why.

Their working hours

Messages wait until it is a weekday morning at the recipient, using their local time, not yours.

One conversation per company

A second address at the same company waits. No one gets three emails from you in a week.

Everything in one place

Zero terminal.

Queue, leads, templates, pipeline, replies, domain health, compliance, analytics, sent log, research and schedule. Each one reads the same files the scripts use.
